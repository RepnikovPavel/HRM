Currently, HRMs appear to work well for mapping fixed-size inputs to fixed-size outputs (e.g. 2D grids).

Do the authors see any potential applications of HRM to sequence-to-sequence modelling with variable input/output lengths, e.g. for generative modelling (machine translation, question answering)? Is this something you've explored already, or are you aware of any ongoing work in this direction?

What would be the main challenges to approaching variable-length inputs/outputs with HRM?

Curious to hear the authors' thoughts on this!
