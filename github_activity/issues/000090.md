### Comment by @alexander-rakhlin at 2025-10-06T22:37:51Z

While the developers are looking for an answer to why their checkpoint isn't loading, I'll answer for them. The problem is caused by the fact that the model architecture is parameterized by the dataset size. Not the dimensionality, but the size.

This error:
```
RuntimeError: Error(s) in loading state_dict for OptimizedModule:
	size mismatch for _orig_mod.model.inner.puzzle_emb.weights: copying a param with shape torch.Size([1045829, 512]) from checkpoint, the shape in current model is torch.Size([1921251, 512]).
```
is caused by the fact that `_orig_mod.model.inner.puzzle_emb` has a dimension of 1921251x512 but is initialized with a size of 1045829x512. 1921251 and 1045829 are the num_puzzle_identifiers that were involved in the training. For some reason, they are different in the developers' dataset and in our dataset. And since they parameterize the model (although they ideally shouldn't), the model breaks. To load the developers' checkpoint, you can replace `"num_puzzle_identifiers": 1921251` with `1045829` in `dataset.json`.
