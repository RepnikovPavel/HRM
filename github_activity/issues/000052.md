### Comment by @kroggen at 2025-08-11T14:38:50Z

Your change ignores `L_cycles` config completely. Now `L_level` is called twice at each step. One before and one after the `H_level`

The `H_level` is not always called at the end of the step. Note that the output of this HierarchicalReasoningModel_ACTV1_Inner block is based on the output of the `H_level`. Depending on the config of `H_cycles` this may cause the last call to the `L_level` to be a no-op (or just update internal state).

And AFAIK the desired result regarding back-prop is not that H_net updates slower than L_net, but to have a faster back-prop that also uses less memory (do not process all previous steps)
